# **App Name**: LaunderSync

## Core Features:

- Order Pickup Management Dashboard: A centralized dashboard to view all pending pickup requests, showing customer names and their locations for efficient scheduling.
- AI-Powered Pickup Message Tool: An AI tool to craft and send personalized SMS or app notifications to customers, including pickup schedules, reminders, and service updates.
- Mpesa Payment Initiation: Integrate Mpesa to allow direct initiation of payment prompts to customers for their laundry service bills.
- Order Lifecycle Tracking: Functionality to update and track the status of each laundry order through various stages, from pickup to completion and delivery.
- Customer Information Management: A system to securely store and retrieve customer contact details, addresses, and past order history for streamlined management.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A deep, professional blue (#3380CC), representing trust and cleanliness.
- Background color: A subtle, desaturated light blue (#F0F2F5), providing a clean and fresh canvas for content.
- Accent color: A vibrant aqua (#52E0E0), used for highlighting interactive elements and calls to action, evoking a sense of efficiency.
- All text will use 'Inter', a versatile sans-serif typeface, for its modern, clean lines and excellent legibility across all content types.
- Utilize a consistent set of line-art icons that visually communicate various actions and categories related to laundry services, such as 'pickup', 'wash', 'dry', and 'deliver'.
- Implement a clean, card-based dashboard layout for clear organization of customer orders and status updates, ensuring responsiveness across devices.
- Incorporate subtle hover effects on actionable items and smooth transitions between dashboard views to enhance user experience without distraction.
